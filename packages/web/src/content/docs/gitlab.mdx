---
title: GitLab
description: Run CYBERVINCI in operator-controlled GitLab CI jobs.
---

This fork does not publish a CYBERVINCI GitLab CI component and does not rename community OpenCode components.

To use CYBERVINCI in GitLab, provision a reviewed CYBERVINCI binary in your own runner and invoke it from a project-owned job. Keep tokens in protected CI variables and apply an explicit job timeout.

Do not use a package or component merely because its name contains CYBERVINCI unless its source and publisher are controlled by your organization.
