---
title: Ecosystem
description: Compatibility policy for third-party OpenCode integrations.
---

CYBERVINCI does not rename or republish third-party OpenCode plugins, extensions, templates, or community projects.

Those projects keep their original proper names and remain independently maintained. Their compatibility with CYBERVINCI has not been certified, so this fork intentionally does not present rewritten or invented CYBERVINCI package URLs.

Use a third-party integration only after reviewing its source and confirming compatibility with this fork.
